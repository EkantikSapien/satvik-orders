Satvik Orders privacy policy

Satvik Orders is the private order book used by the Satvik Catering team in Brisbane. Only people the business has added by email can sign in.

What the app uses

When you sign in with Google, the app reads your name and email address to check that you are on the team and to show who entered each order, expense or payment.

Where information is kept

Orders, expenses and payments are saved in a Google Sheet, and receipt photos in a Google Drive folder, both in the business owner's Google Drive. The app can only access files it created itself (Google's “drive.file” permission). It cannot see anything else in anyone's Google Drive.

Sharing

Information is not sold or shared with anyone outside Satvik Catering. It is hosted on Vercel and stored by Google.

Removing access or data

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. To ask about or remove information, contact patelpranjal07@gmail.com.
